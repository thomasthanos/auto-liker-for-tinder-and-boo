# Privacy policy - Auto Liker for Tinder & Boo

**Last updated:** 24 August 2026  
**Publisher:** Thomas Thanos

Nothing leaves your browser and nothing is saved. The extension has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, account system, or remote data service.

The extension has no `storage` permission and makes no network requests. Its like counter exists only in the background worker's memory and disappears when the browser closes.

It does not read or store profiles, messages, matches, or personal information. It locates the relevant on-page control and activates it. The content script is limited to `tinder.com` and `boo.world`.

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in its update date and in the public commit history.
